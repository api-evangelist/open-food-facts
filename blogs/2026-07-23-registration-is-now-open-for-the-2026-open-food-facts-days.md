Registration is now open! The Open Food Facts Days 2026 will bring together citizens, researchers, contributors and curious minds for two days dedicated to food transparency, open data and citizen science. Whether you’re a long-time contributor or simply someone who wants to understand what’s really in your food, this gathering is for you!
